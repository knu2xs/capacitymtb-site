Whether you're a student looking for adventure, a parent searching for a positive activity, an experienced rider interested in coaching, or a local business looking to support youth in our community, we'd love to have you join us.

## Athletes

Capital City MTB is a place to ride bikes, make friends, challenge yourself, and discover what you're capable of.

Our athletes come from a wide range of backgrounds and experience levels. Some arrive with years of mountain biking experience, while others are completely new to the sport. Some are motivated by racing and competition. Others simply enjoy exploring trails, spending time outdoors, and being part of a team. There is a place for all of them here.

The trails surrounding Olympia provide an incredible environment for learning and progression. From the rolling terrain of the Black Hills to the extensive trail networks of Capital Forest, our athletes have access to some of the finest riding opportunities in the Pacific Northwest. More importantly, they have a community of coaches and teammates ready to support them every step of the way.

You do not need to be the fastest rider. You do not need expensive equipment. You do not need prior racing experience.

You simply need a willingness to ride, learn, and have fun.

If you're looking for an opportunity to build confidence, develop new skills, spend time outdoors, and become part of an amazing community, we invite you to join us on the trail.

## Coaches

Capital City Mountain Bike Team is made possible through the incredible efforts of our volunteer coaches.

Every practice, ride, and event depends upon dedicated adults who generously share their time, experience, and encouragement with our athletes. Coaches are far more than ride leaders. They are mentors, teachers, role models, mechanics, problem-solvers, cheerleaders, and trusted adults who help young people grow both on and off the bike.

The impact of a great coach extends well beyond mountain biking. Young athletes may not remember every trail they ride or every race they complete, but they often remember the adults who believed in them, encouraged them through challenges, and helped them discover confidence they didn't know they possessed.

You do not need to be a professional rider to coach. Some of our best coaches are simply people who care deeply about helping young people succeed. The Washington Student Cycling League provides training, resources, and support to help volunteers become safe and effective coaches.

If you enjoy mountain biking, value youth development, and want to make a meaningful difference in our community, we would love to talk with you about joining our coaching team.

## Sponsors & Community Partners

Strong community programs are built through strong community support.

Capital City MTB is proud to be supported by local businesses, organizations, and individuals who believe in providing opportunities for young people to thrive outdoors. These partnerships help us reduce barriers to participation, support team activities, develop future coaches, and ensure that more youth have access to the benefits of mountain biking.

Our sponsors do more than support a sports team. They invest in healthy lifestyles, outdoor recreation, leadership development, community connection, and the next generation of trail stewards and outdoor enthusiasts.

We are grateful for every individual and organization that chooses to support our athletes and volunteers. Together, we are helping create opportunities that will positively impact young people for years to come.

If your business or organization is interested in supporting youth mountain biking in our community, we would welcome the opportunity to connect.
