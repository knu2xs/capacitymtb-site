# Welcome to Capital City Mountain Bike Team

The Capital City Mountain Bike Team exists to help young people discover the joy of riding bikes, exploring trails, building friendships, and challenging themselves in the outdoors.

We are a youth mountain bike team based in Olympia and participate in the Washington Student Cycling League, a statewide organization dedicated to getting more kids on bikes in a fun, safe, and supportive environment. While we participate in league events and races throughout the season, our focus extends far beyond competition. We believe mountain biking offers an incredible opportunity for young people to develop confidence, resilience, independence, and a lifelong appreciation for the outdoors.

At its heart, our team is about people.

Every season brings together student-athletes, volunteer coaches, parents, and community members who share a common goal: creating a welcoming environment where every rider can grow. Some athletes join with years of trail experience. Others may be riding dirt for the first time. Some are motivated by racing and competition, while others simply enjoy spending time outdoors with friends. All are welcome.

One of the things that makes our team special is where we ride. Olympia is fortunate to be surrounded by exceptional trail systems that provide opportunities for riders of all skill levels. Capital Forest offers hundreds of miles of roads, trails, and singletrack that have earned a reputation among mountain bikers throughout the Pacific Northwest. Closer to town, the Black Hills provide additional opportunities for riders to build skills and confidence in a setting that feels both accessible and adventurous.

These trails become more than places to ride. They become classrooms for learning perseverance when the climb gets steep, patience when a skill takes time to master, and humility when there is always something new to learn. They become places where friendships are formed and where young people discover that they are capable of more than they imagined.

None of this would be possible without the volunteers who give generously of their time, energy, and experience. Our coaches are mentors, teachers, role models, and advocates for every athlete on the team. They work hard to create an environment where riders feel supported, encouraged, and challenged to become their best selves both on and off the bike.
